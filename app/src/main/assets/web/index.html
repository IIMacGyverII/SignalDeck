DECODE A RECORDING

          
A pass recording (Satellite passes → Record each pass), a REC recording, or one from anywhere (Upload…: WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG, FLAC, up to 200 MB), decoded on the phone in the background, faster than it plays; the radio is not used. SSTV finds every mode from its header (Robot, Martin, Scottie, PD…) and Robot 36 pictures whose header was lost. Pictures join the gallery, packets are listed below.

          

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
BAND SCOPE

        
Receive only: the radio sweeps the band step by step (about a second a step: its signal reading needs 0.7 s after each retune) and comes back to your channel when it stops. Pick a band or a custom range; click the graph to pick a frequency.

TRANSCRIPTS 

          

        
        
Every finished caption sentence the phone kept, newest first, with its time, channel and (on DMR) the caller. New ones appear as they are heard. Captions are made on the phone: turn TXT on here or on the phone.

        

      

      

        
CAPTION ENGINE

        

        

        
On the phone: speech is recognised on the phone itself, nothing leaves it. The model is downloaded once (over the phone's connection) and kept; bigger models are more accurate but need more space and memory. Google: your own API key, audio is sent to Google.

TOOLS

            

          
PACKET FINDER

            
Listens across the packet frequencies (never transmits) and lists the Winlink gateways, BBSs and nodes it hears. Nodes identify only every 10 to 30 minutes: let it run an hour or two, or schedule it for the night. The radio goes back to its channel afterwards.

            

            

              
              
              
              

            

            

          
VOICE KEYER

            
Upload a recording (any audio file; it is converted to the radio's 8 kHz and cut at 25 s), play it here, then Send it on the current analog channel. Messages recorded on the phone show up here too.

M17 RECEIVE

            

            

            

            

            

            
M17 digital voice on the phone's current channel or VFO: the caller, who it is for and their message; press Listen on the Intercom page to hear the decoded voice. Receive only (this radio cannot transmit M17). 

          
FT8 OVER FM

            

              
              

            

            
            

            

            

            
The WSJT-X mode on the phone's current FM channel. Click a CQ to answer it: the phone runs the exchange and logs the contact. It cannot hear SSB FT8 (144.174 / 432.174), and FM lets one station through per slot. Your callsign shows as (you). Sending needs Allow transmit from the web.

SCHEDULED OPERATIONS

        
Start something at a set time, once or every week; when the time is up the radio goes back to what it was doing. A satellite pass being tracked comes first.

        

        

          
          
          
          

          
          
          
          
          

        

      

      

        

          RADIO
          

          

          
Take over gives the radio to SignalDeck; Hand back returns it to the stock walkie-talkie app (and this page loses the radio until you take it over again).

          RADIO MODULE
          

          

          
Restart and installing an update each ask you to confirm. The firmware loader, Own the radio at boot and Allow unlisted images stay on the phone.

          THIS CONNECTION
          

          
Web access, the PIN and “Allow transmit from the web” are changed only on the phone (Device → SignalDeck Web), so a browser can never give itself more rights.

          BACKUP AND RESTORE
          

          

          
          
A restore replaces every channel, contact and zone with the backup; history, messages and settings are kept.
